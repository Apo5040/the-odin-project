An upside-down caramelized pineapple cake is a classic, moist dessert featuring a beautiful golden topping of caramelized fruit.

Baked with rings of sweet pineapple laid over a rich homemade caramel, it is topped with a soft, buttery sponge batter spiked with rum. Once flipped onto a plate, the caramelized juices soak back into the cake for an incredibly juicy and flavorful finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 can of pineapple slices
  • 150g of sugar + 150g for the caramel
  • 150g of flour
  • 150g of butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 45g of rum

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Mix the softened butter with the sugars.
  3. Add the eggs, flour, baking powder and the brandy.
  4. In a pan, make the caramel without adding water.
  5. Once golden brown, pour it into the cake mold, then add the pineapple slices, and finally the batter.
  6. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 35 minutes.
  7. Unmold and flip upside down onto a plate.
