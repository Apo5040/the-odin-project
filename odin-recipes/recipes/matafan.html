Matafan

An apple Matafan (or Matefaim) is a traditional, rustic French thick pancake from "Les Alpes" region, loaded with caramelized apple slices.

Made from a simple batter of flour, eggs, sugar, and milk, it is slowly cooked in a buttered skillet until golden and fluffy, then flipped to form a comforting, cake-like dessert. Its name literally translates to "hunger-killer".

Ingredients

  • 4 pommes
  • 125gr de farine
  • 70gr de sucre
  • 15 gr d'eau de vie
  • 15 gr de beurre
  • 2 oeufs
  • 10gr d'eau tiède
  • 1 sachet de levure
  • 1 sachet de sucre vanillé
  • 15gr de cannelle
  • 0.5gr de sel

Steps

  1. Peel and slice the apples.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugars, baking powder, and a pinch of salt. Add the eggs, then the brandy (eau-de-vie) and lukewarm water. Whisk well until smooth.
  3. Butter a large skillet, including the sides. Sauté the apple slices until they are beautifully golden brown.
  4. Pour in the batter to completely cover the apples. Cook this "omelet" until the bottom is golden brown.
  5. Flip the Matafan onto a serving plate and dust with cinnamon.
  6. To be enjoyed hot, warm, or chilled.
